📖 The Game Story

🌴 Lost in the Jungle 🌴

You've crash-landed in a mysterious jungle and discovered an ancient mini-golf course. Legend says that completing all three holes is the only way to escape!


Your Mission: Navigate through three increasingly challenging holes, mastering different clubs and avoiding jungle obstacles to achieve the best score and find your way home.

⛳
Hole 1: The Beginning
A gentle introduction with basic obstacles. Learn the controls and get a feel for the clubs.
🌊
Hole 2: Water Hazards
Navigate around dangerous water traps and through narrow passages.
🏛️
Hole 3: The Temple
Face the ultimate challenge - precise shots through ancient ruins to escape!

💡 Ideation & Planning

Initial Concept

  • Create an engaging 3D golf experience
  • Focus on realistic physics and feel
  • Jungle theme for visual appeal
  • Progressive difficulty across holes

Key Decisions

  • Custom Physics: Better control for golf mechanics
  • 4 Clubs: Driver, Iron, Wedge, Putter
  • 3D Golfer: Visual feedback for player
  • Dynamic Camera: Follows ball flight

Core Game Loop Design:

1. Player aims with mouse/keyboard
2. Selects appropriate club (1-4 keys)
3. Holds mouse to charge power (0-100%)
4. Ball launches with realistic arc physics
5. Camera follows ball smoothly
6. Collision detection with obstacles
7. Score tracking and hole progression

Physics Engine Rationale:
Custom physics provide precise control over arcade-style golf mechanics. Generic physics engines would require complex tuning for club-specific trajectories, backspin effects, and the precise ball behavior needed for an enjoyable golf experience. Our custom implementation is simpler, more performant, and gives exact control over game feel.

✅ Requirements: Interaction & Physics

Player Controls

  • Mouse: Click+drag to aim, hold to charge power
  • Arrow Keys / A/D: Rotate camera view
  • Q/E: Zoom in/out
  • 1/2/3/4: Select club
  • Spacebar: Hit ball
  • ESC: Pause menu

Physics & Movement

  • Gravity: 9.81 m/s² realistic fall
  • Velocity: 3D vector physics
  • Arc Trajectories: Realistic ball flight
  • Backspin: Club-specific effects
  • Collision Detection: Dual-check system
  • Energy Retention: 40% on bounce
Collision Detection System:

Dual-Position Check: Prevents ball from tunneling through obstacles at high speed
• Check current position for collisions
• Check next frame position for collisions
• If collision detected: apply 40% energy retention, push ball back 1.5 units
• Penetration resolution prevents getting stuck in objects

🔧 Technical Implementation

Architecture Overview

Class Structure:

Golfer Class - 3D character representation
• Body, head, arms, club meshes
• Positioned 0.6 units to side of ball
• Pendulum swing animation (3 phases)

Golf Class - Ball physics and player control
• Ball physics with velocity, gravity, arc
• hitBall(power, direction) method
• Collision detection system
• Club management (4 clubs)

Jungle Class - Environment and scene management
• createHole() - Generates each hole layout
• clearScene() - Cleanup between holes
• Obstacle placement and management
• Hole indicator (animated arrow + ring)

4 Club System

Driver: 40u distance, high arc
Iron: 25u distance, med arc
Wedge: 15u distance, low arc
Putter: 10u distance, flat

Camera Modes

Player: 2u behind, 1.2u high
Follow: Dynamic 6-8u zoom
Hole: 10u overview
Auto-switch: Smart transitions
